## CRUD
All forms:
- React Hook Form
- Zod
- clear validation
- optimistic UI only for non-critical content
- server confirmation for mutations

## QR management
Create experience → Generate QR → preview → download/print.

## Analytics
Use date/event filters and aggregate queries.

## Attendee management
Search by VIBE ID/name. Admin can view profile and ledger. Balance adjustments require reason and audit record.

## Staff
Assign staff to zones. Staff cannot self-assign.

## Dangerous actions
Delete/deactivate content with confirmation. Prefer soft deactivation during a live event.
